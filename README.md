

# Hi, I'm Dr. Kriti Yadav 👋

Researcher in applied mathematics and scientific machine learning, working on **mathematical models of atmospheric vortices** and **physics-informed deep learning for tropical cyclones**.

- 🎓 I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences at **IIT (BHU) Varanasi**.
- 🌀 My research covers analytical viscous models of tropical cyclones, dust devils and whirlwinds, and **physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)** for reconstructing cyclone structure from reanalysis data.
- 🌱 I'm currently expanding into Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing.
- 🤝 Open to collaborations on **AI for fluid dynamics, weather and climate**.
- 💼 Available for freelance work in machine learning, data science and scientific computing.
